NIMBUS ANALYTICS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Last updated: March 2026

SECTION 1: WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours at Nimbus Analytics are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees on the Engineering and Data Science teams may opt into flexible hours, arriving anytime between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided they complete an 8-hour workday and attend the daily 11:00 AM standup meeting.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week without prior approval. Fully remote arrangements require sign-off from both the employee's manager and the Head of People Operations. Remote employees must be reachable via Slack during core hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time) and must attend in-person quarterly planning sessions held at the Austin headquarters.

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days. PTO increases to 22 days after three years of continuous employment and to 25 days after six years. Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the following calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

SECTION 4: PARENTAL LEAVE
Nimbus Analytics offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, regardless of gender, applicable to birth, adoption, or foster placement. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and can be split into two blocks with manager approval.

SECTION 5: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses under $75 may be submitted through the Expensify app without a manager's pre-approval, but still require a receipt. Expenses over $75, and all travel bookings, require written pre-approval from a direct manager. Reimbursement requests must be filed within 30 days of the expense date; requests filed later are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by Finance.

SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews occur twice a year, in April and October. Reviews combine self-assessment, peer feedback from at least three colleagues, and a manager evaluation. Compensation adjustments, when applicable, are announced within 30 days of the October review cycle and take effect the following January 1st.

SECTION 7: EQUIPMENT POLICY
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home-office stipend during their first 60 days. Equipment is refreshed every three years or upon documented failure. Employees who leave the company must return all hardware within 14 days of their last day, or the equipment's depreciated value will be deducted from their final paycheck where legally permitted.
